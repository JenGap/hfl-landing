THE PROBLEM

Fiber content is only the beginning — dyes, finishes, coatings, and treatments all matter, and almost no one has a trusted place to learn about any of it.

Healthy Fabric Library is building the public infrastructure that turns complex material science into information people can understand, experience, and act on.

What your gift builds

Three connected pieces, moving from concept into a public, testable resource this year.

The Healthy Fabric Database

A public-facing platform translating material science into plain language — fibers, dyes, finishes, certifications, and healthier alternatives, seed to skin to soil.

Material Matchmaking

Connecting designers and brands directly with vetted fiber innovators, mills, dye makers, and manufacturers who can deliver on their values.

Mobile Immersive Exhibit

Taking material science into communities — visitors touch materials, investigate clothing ingredients, and experience emerging fiber technologies firsthand.
